# Three-minute demo

1. Start the app and explain the distinction between six metadata studies and zero measured runs.
2. Search FLEX, open its source evidence, and follow the NASA section locator.
3. Clear search, select BASS and FLEX, then compare. Point out unknown materials/hardware.
4. Open Scenario, choose lunar habitat/lunar gravity and evaluate. Inspect unknown components, mismatch flags and lower/upper relevance bounds.
5. Ask the assistant about FLEX; open the source summary and explain retrieval-only mode.
6. Export JSON or printable HTML. Check source URLs, the scenario and methodology version.

Do not claim identification of a safest material or quantitative fire probability.
